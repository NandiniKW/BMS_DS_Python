Part 2: Concise Biology Cheat Sheet for Data Science Instructors Non-Biology Primer

A practical translation of biological lab concepts into data types, physical mechanics, and quantitative operations.

1. Cell Counting & Hemocytometer int

Physical Meaning: A hemocytometer is a thick glass microscope slide with a precision-etched grid. Researchers manually tally cells in each grid square under a microscope.

Data Science Logic: Living cells cannot be cut in half; they are strictly discrete count data (Poisson-distributed integers).

Aliquoting (// and %): Taking a 1,003 µL bulk solution and distributing it across 4 reaction tubes: 1003 // 4 = 250 µL per tube, leaving 1003 % 4 = 3 µL of leftover "dead volume".

2. $OD_{600}$ & Continuous Assays float

Physical Meaning: A spectrophotometer beams 600 nm wavelength light through liquid bacteria. As cells divide, the liquid grows turbid/cloudy and scatters light.

Data Science Logic: Higher $OD_{600}$ = higher bacterial cell density. Because spectrophotometers and pH electrodes measure continuous analog voltages, they map to Python floating-point values requiring precision rounding.

3. DNA Sequences & Polarity str

Chemical Alphabet: DNA polymers are chains composed of 4 chemical letters: Adenine (A), Thymine (T), Guanine (G), and Cytosine (C).

Directional Polarity (5' → 3'): DNA has asymmetrical chemical ends named 5' ("five-prime") and 3' ("three-prime"). Text strings represent DNA from 5' to 3':

  • seq[:5] is the 5' terminal flank.
  • seq[-5:] is the 3' terminal flank.

4. Codons & Transcription Methods

Codons (3-letter tokens): Ribosomes read RNA in triplets to assemble amino acids. ATG is the universal start codon (initiator). TAA, TAG, and TGA are stop codons (terminators).

In Silico Transcription: Cells copy DNA into mRNA by swapping Thymine for Uracil (U): 

mrna = dna.replace("T", "U")

5. GC Content (%) Formula

Formula: $ ext{GC\%} = rac{ ext{G count} + ext{C count}}{ ext{Total Sequence Length}} imes 100$

Why it Matters: A–T pairs share 2 hydrogen bonds; G–C pairs share 3 hydrogen bonds. Sequences with higher GC content require higher temperatures to denature/melt. Biologists calculate GC% to design reliable PCR primers.

6. Replicates & Metadata list & dict

Biological Replicates (list): Living systems are intrinsically noisy. Assays are run in duplicates or triplicates (e.g. [0.42, 0.58, 0.71]) to compute means and standard errors.

Clinical Metadata (dict): An assay number is useless without context. A dictionary maps sample keys ("id", "treatment", "temp") to experimental conditions.

7. Differential Gene Expression & Pathway Overlap set

Biological Scenario: Exposing cells to distinct stressors (e.g. Hypoxia vs. Oxidative Stress) activates different defense genes.

Set Logic in Genomics:

  • Union (.union()): The complete pan-experiment catalogue of responsive biomarkers.
  • Intersection (.intersection()): The common stress-response core shared by both conditions.
  • Difference (.difference()): Biomarkers unique and specific to a single condition.
